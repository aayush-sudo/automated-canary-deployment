- No Chaos Mesh resource is created by the Jenkinsfile.

Proposed Jenkins trigger
Use the existing Jenkins job's Build with Parameters action:

CANARY_IMAGE=canary-demo:v3
DEPLOY_MODE=ROLLOUT_V3

If the Jenkins endpoint and credentials are already configured, the
equivalent parameterized HTTP trigger is:

curl.exe -X POST "<JENKINS_URL>/job/<JOB_NAME>/buildWithParameters" `
  --data-urlencode "CANARY_IMAGE=canary-demo:v3" `
  --data-urlencode "DEPLOY_MODE=ROLLOUT_V3"

The URL and authentication must be supplied by the Jenkins environment; they
were not inferred or executed here.

Expected changes after approval
- Jenkins builds and loads canary-demo:v3 into devops-canary.
- Jenkins patches the Flagger target template to create a new candidate
  revision.
- Flagger creates canary-demo-flagger-canary and temporarily shifts traffic.
- The existing healthy primary is retained while analysis runs.
- Flagger owns the generated VirtualService weights.
- This does not apply NetworkChaos.

Important timing constraint
- The Jenkins rollout stage waits for promotion and may finish after the
  candidate has been promoted and removed.
- Candidate label, readiness, routing, and latency must therefore be checked
  while the Jenkins rollout is in progress, before it completes.
- If the candidate cannot be observed during the rollout, stop and diagnose
  before applying any chaos.
